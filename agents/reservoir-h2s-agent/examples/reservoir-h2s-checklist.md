# Reservoir H2S Checklist

A walkthrough for estimating H2S in the gas of a prospect or discovery before production. Public methods only; bring your own calibration data.

## 1. Scope

- [ ] Question stated: expected value, design value, or plausibility of a reading.
- [ ] Basis stated: test-separator gas, ppm by mole, before production.

## 2. Prospect data

- [ ] Reservoir temperature (or depth and the temperature trend used), pressure, CO2, fluid type and position collected.
- [ ] Depositional setting resolved from the formation; red beds, carbonates and cold reservoirs flagged.
- [ ] Missing items listed as data gaps; how well each input is known is stated.

## 3. Buffer

- [ ] Buffer solved at reservoir conditions and flashed to separator gas; fluid basis stated.
- [ ] Siderite and pyrite stability margins positive.

## 4. Estimate

- [ ] Registry and well table identified (source, date, number of wells per setting).
- [ ] P10/P50/P90, exceedance probabilities and the range for design reported.
- [ ] Uncertainty budget reported; its largest term named.
- [ ] Readings of the discovery, if any, compared with the buffer estimate.

## 5. Screening and review

- [ ] H2S partial pressure at reservoir pressure compared with 0.3 kPa.
- [ ] Gas limit stated with its source; comparison marked as test-separator gas.
- [ ] Souring by injected seawater stated as not included.
- [ ] Human review recorded before use in a design basis.
