# Example prompts — firewater-coverage-agent

## Establishing the demand

> Area is 510 m² on an open weather deck at +50.5 m with nine shell-and-tube gas coolers.
> Governing rate is 10 (l/min)/m². What is the fire-water demand, and how does dedicated
> protection of the coolers compare with blanket area coverage?

## Sizing the nozzle net

> I need 10 (l/min)/m² over 510 m² using an HV-class nozzle with K = 42.9 (l/min)/√bar,
> minimum 3.5 barg, maximum spacing 3.0 m, running at 5 barg. How many nozzles, what grid
> pitch, and which criterion governs?

## Testing a monitor proposal

> Somebody wants four 4000 l/min monitors instead of a fixed deluge net over that deck.
> Design wind is 15 m/s and the droplets fall about 12 m. Does that meet the requirement?

## Testing a passive-protection proposal

> The coolers already sit on a Chartek-coated frame. Can we argue that the passive fire
> protection removes the need for deluge over the area?

## Testing whether the existing system can take it

> The serving deluge skid has a −0.9 bar pressure margin in the two-pump design case, but the
> pumps have about 1000 m³/h of spare flow. Can we add a new 306 m³/h section to that skid?

## Framing a verification finding

> A TTS finding says this area lacks an active extinguishing system, citing NORSOK S-001
> §21.4.1 and §21.4.4. The platform's own firewater basis says general area protection is not
> provided above the coolers. How do I frame that conflict?
