# Validation scenarios

- Digital PDF with embedded text and a chart routes to native extraction plus page rendering and vision.
- Scanned PDF routes to OCR and vision.
- XLSX preserves sheet and cell locators.
- Low-confidence and safety-critical facts require review.
- Conflicting normalized values from two sources remain unresolved and appear in the conflict register.
- Unsupported files stop at manual triage without inferred content.
