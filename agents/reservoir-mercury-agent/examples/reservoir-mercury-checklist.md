# Reservoir Mercury Checklist

A walkthrough for estimating mercury in the gas of a prospect or discovery. Public methods only; bring your own calibration data.

## 1. Scope

- [ ] Question stated: expected value, worst case, or plausibility of a measurement.
- [ ] Gas basis and unit stated (separator or test gas, microgram per Sm3).

## 2. Prospect data

- [ ] Position, formation or epoch, reservoir temperature and pressure, fluid type and CO2 collected.
- [ ] Reservoir age class resolved; missing items listed as data gaps.

## 3. Limits

- [ ] Mineral limits calculated at reservoir conditions with UMR-PRU-MC and expressed in separator gas.
- [ ] Trace-sulfide limit named as worst case; cinnabar and liquid mercury as upper bounds.

## 4. Estimate

- [ ] Registry and test table identified (source, date, number of tests and wells).
- [ ] Highest gas reading used when the discovery has measurements.
- [ ] P10/P50/P90, exceedance probabilities and the range for design reported.

## 5. Accuracy and review

- [ ] Cross-validated error and no-predictor score quoted.
- [ ] A measurement below the P10 of the statistical estimate questioned.
- [ ] Human review recorded before use in a design basis.
