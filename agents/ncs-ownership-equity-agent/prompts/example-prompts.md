# Example prompts

## Partners of a discovery
Who owns Wisting today? List the partners with interest and the operator, the owning licence, and the date Sodir last updated the data.

## Net economics
Aker BP's share of Wisting: take a gross capex of 12000 MNOK in 2028 and 18000 MNOK in 2029 and give Aker BP's net capex. Check that all partners add up to the gross.

## Portfolio
List every field and discovery where Aker BP holds an interest, largest first, and flag discoveries that sit inside a producing field.

## Back-cast
Show how Alvheim equity changed from 2010 to today and give the net production split for 2018.

## Prospect
I have a prospect in licence 537 with a 60/40 split between Opco and Partner. Set it up as a prospect with my source, and also show what the public licence data says so I can see the difference.
