# Ownership and equity checklist

- [ ] Asset type identified (field, discovery, licence, prospect) and name resolved to one object.
- [ ] `as_of` date stated; back-cast dates listed when used.
- [ ] Owner named: production licence or unit (business arrangement area).
- [ ] Interests add up to 100 %, or the shortfall is explained.
- [ ] A discovery inside a field is evaluated through the field's equity.
- [ ] Prospect equity is user-provided (with source) or a labelled licence proxy.
- [ ] Sodir update date quoted; pending transfers or carries confirmed with the user.
- [ ] Net series add up to gross across all partners.
- [ ] Tax computed per company on net cash flow, not scaled from a gross figure.
- [ ] Facts, assumptions and recommendations separated; human review recorded.
