# Example Prompts — Emissions Abatement Screening Agent

Use public data only. Keep every reused figure with its source and reference year.

## Carbon-cost basis

```text
Give me the public Norwegian carbon-cost basis for 2025 with source attribution: the CO2 tax per Sm3 gas and per tonne CO2, the gas-venting tax, the EU ETS allowance cost, the combined effective CO2 cost, and the NOx Fund rate. Note the reference year and that rates must be re-verified against the source.
```

## Emissions baseline

```text
Estimate a screening baseline of field-life energy use and CO2e emissions and a carbon intensity for an NCS gas platform burning about 60 MSm3/year of fuel gas in turbines. Report the CO2-by-source context and keep every figure with its source and reference year.
```

## Single-measure abatement screening

```text
Screen a waste-heat-recovery measure that avoids 20 MSm3/year of fuel gas for a CAPEX of 300 MNOK. Using the 2025 Norwegian carbon-cost basis and a gas price of 2 NOK/Sm3, a 15-year horizon, and an 8% discount rate, report avoided carbon cost, avoided fuel value, net annual saving, simple payback, NPV, and the breakeven CO2 price with a verdict.
```

## Compare and rank measures

```text
Rank two emission-reduction measures for an NCS platform on NPV and payback: (a) waste-heat recovery avoiding 20 MSm3/year of fuel gas for 300 MNOK CAPEX, and (b) power-from-shore avoiding 200 kt CO2/year for 8000 MNOK CAPEX with 150 MNOK/year of imported-power cost. Use the 2025 carbon-cost basis, name the validated NeqSim energy and economics workflows, and prepare a screening report outline with assumptions and limitations.
```

## Breakeven carbon price

```text
For a compressor-efficiency upgrade avoiding 8 MSm3/year of fuel gas at a CAPEX of 120 MNOK over a 12-year horizon at 8%, what CO2 price (NOK/tonne) would make the measure break even? Compare it to the current combined Norwegian CO2 cost and state whether the measure is attractive today.
```
