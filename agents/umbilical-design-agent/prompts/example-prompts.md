# Example Prompts

These prompts use public, synthetic or approved data only. The agent performs screening and always requires qualified human review.

## Size a control umbilical

```text
Size a 15 km control umbilical in 100 m water depth for a template tie-back: hydraulic lines 9.5 mm and 6.35 mm ID at 345 and 690 bar, MEG 25.4 mm at 0.5 m3/h, methanol 12.7 mm at 0.3 m3/h, a 3-core 16 mm2 3.3 kV power cable carrying 50 kW and a 12-fibre tube. Report the design checks and draw the cross-section.
```

## Check a chemical line against the flow-assurance demand

```text
The flow-assurance hand-off needs 0.8 m3/h of lean MEG at the template. Check whether a 25.4 mm ID line over 15 km can deliver it within half of its 345 bar design pressure at 4 C, and give the smallest inner diameter that passes.
```

## Hydraulic response

```text
How long does a hydraulic control line of 9.5 mm ID take to reach 90 % of a topside pressure step at the template 15 km away? Compare with a 30 s valve response requirement and suggest options if it fails.
```

## Power step-out

```text
Check the power cable for a 1.5 MW load over 25 km at 6.6 kV with 35 mm2 conductors. Report voltage drop, loss and charging current, and show how they change with length.
```

## Report figures

```text
Prepare the umbilical figures and hand-off for the study report with basis version 2026-11-13: cross-section, hydraulics, tension, power and the one-page summary, with captions and the list of open items.
```
