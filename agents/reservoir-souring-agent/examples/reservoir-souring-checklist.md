# Reservoir Souring Checklist

A walkthrough for screening H2S development in a waterflooded field from measured gas H2S. Public methods only; use measured, consistent data.

## 1. Scope

- [ ] Wells, period and objective stated (correlation per well, plausibility check, mitigation screen).
- [ ] Gas measurement stage, ppmv basis and sample times documented.

## 2. Gas to wellstream

- [ ] Separator pressure and temperature at sample time recorded.
- [ ] Wellstream composition and water mole fraction from the same period.
- [ ] Conversion round-trips and H2S balance closes within 2 percent.
- [ ] Conversion-factor curve versus water fraction produced and reviewed.

## 3. Seawater fraction

- [ ] Conservative tracer (Cl, Mg, Br); end members documented; sulfate and barium not used.
- [ ] Multi-tracer residual small; outliers explained.

## 4. Correlations

- [ ] At least five points per well; range of seawater fraction spans the prediction use.
- [ ] Best model by AICc recorded with R2 and warnings; low-confidence wells flagged.
- [ ] Registry saved; previous version kept; change warnings reviewed after each update.

## 5. Plausibility and mitigation

- [ ] Correlation checked against mixing-zone bound and sulfate balance.
- [ ] Thermal cutoff, sink and mitigation factors stated.
- [ ] Sour-service pH2S screen reported and handed to materials engineering.

## 6. Review

- [ ] Assumptions, limits and follow-up studies listed.
- [ ] Qualified human review completed.
