# Example: Frontier Block Bid Screening (Public Synthetic Block)

This example uses a public, synthetic block. No confidential data is used.

## Opportunity

- Block with 30% chance of discovery, 70% working interest, signature bonus 20 MUSD, exploration programme 60 MUSD.
- Concept A: tie-back to a nearby FPSO. Concept B: stand-alone FPSO.
- Oil price 70 USD/bbl, discount rate 10%, generic placeholder fiscal terms.

## Screening summary (illustrative)

| Driver | Concept A (tie-back) | Concept B (stand-alone) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Development CAPEX | lower | higher |
| EMV at the offered share | higher | lower |
| Break-even profit-oil share | higher | lower |
| Share headroom | larger | smaller |

## Interpretation

The tie-back leaves more room above the offered share. The stand-alone concept only creates value if volumes reach the high case, so it is the concept to stress with the volume and price sensitivities.

All values are illustrative. Run the validated NeqSim field-development and economics classes before any decision.
